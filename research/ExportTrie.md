# Export trie research

Apple Mach-O loader.h (https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/main/EXTERNAL_HEADERS/mach-o/loader.h), dyld MachOFile.cpp (https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/dyld/blob/main/common/MachOFile.cpp), and LLVM's MachOObjectFile.cpp (https://llvm.org/doxygen/MachOObjectFile_8cpp_source.html) document trie terminals, library ordinals, renamed/same-name reexports and both load-command representations. Schema 9 adds bounded read-only export summaries and records whole-library reexport declarations. Standalone CLI: python3 scripts/export_trie.py IMAGE --symbol '_name'. Missing entries mean not observed, never system-wide absence. Addresses and resolver functions are not evaluated. Unsupported flags or malformed tables fail conservatively. No cache extraction, cache address translation, recursive resolution, legacy export fallback, loader modification or binary rewriting.
