# Classic binding research

Apple loader.h defines the regular, lazy, weak/coalescing and threaded bind opcode formats: https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/main/EXTERNAL_HEADERS/mach-o/loader.h . The new reader inventories ordinary and lazy bind names, symbol weak-import flags and signed special ordinals without performing relocations or executing code. Repeated bind opcodes are counted with bounds checks but not expanded into address lists. Each stream is atomic: invalid or unsupported streams remain uninspected. Weak coalescing records may describe definitions and are explicitly excluded from the import queue; threaded streams remain unsupported. The analyzer retains its conservative existing complete flag and adds per-stream status. No actual app or firmware is included. Synthetic tests only until independent compiler fixtures verify this reader. Foundation URL parsing changed in iOS 17; Apple documents RFC 3986 and explicit invalid-character encoding at https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/url/init(string:encodinginvalidcharacters:) . Weak imports alone do not prove fallback or absence; no URL alias implemented.
